# Multi-site selective (community-normalized) ARG removal — conventional treatment

Selective log10 removal = log10(influent_norm / effluent_norm); positive = reduced relative to community, <=0 = unchanged or ENRICHED.

Sites: Bliesen (Germany, qPCR 16S-norm, this study) | Taiwan, Slovakia (qPCR 16S-norm, PMC11471163).

## Selective log10 removal by ARG class x site (qPCR)

| arg_class    |   Bliesen |   Taiwan |   Slovakia |
|:-------------|----------:|---------:|-----------:|
| sulfonamide  |      0.18 |    -0.05 |      -0.24 |
| tetracycline |      0.79 |     0.36 |      -0.16 |
| MLS          |      1.21 |     0.7  |       0.08 |
| beta-lactam  |      0.61 |    -0.31 |       0.59 |

## Fraction of (class x site) cases with NO selective removal (<=0)

- 4/12 site×class cases show no selective removal (ARG unchanged or enriched relative to the community).
- Mean selective removal across all cases: 0.31 log10.

## Interpretation

Conventional treatment does not reliably reduce the community-normalized resistome across sites; several ARG classes are unchanged or enriched in effluent. This generalizes the single-site finding that bulk ARG removal is largely biomass-driven, and motivates advanced quaternary treatment — which (at Bliesen) achieves selective removal but still spares the mobile integron.
